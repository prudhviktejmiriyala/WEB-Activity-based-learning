Nutritious Recipes

Fresh Vegetable Salad

A refreshing mix of vegetables and healthy dressing.

Calories: Approximately 180 kcal/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 carrot
  • Lemon juice and salt

Preparation: Chop the vegetables, mix them together, and add lemon juice.

Fruit and Oats Bowl

A nourishing breakfast with oats and fresh fruit.

Calories: Approximately 300 kcal/serving

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 banana
  • Chopped seasonal fruit

Preparation: Cook the oats in milk and top with sliced fruit.

Vegetable Soup

A warm soup prepared with colorful vegetables.

Calories: Approximately 150 kcal/serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 2 cups water or vegetable stock
  • Garlic and pepper
  • Salt to taste

Preparation: Cook the vegetables with stock and season with pepper.
